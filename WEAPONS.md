# Weapon System Guide

## Available Guns

### 1. **Pistol** 🔫
- **Fire Rate**: Fast (0.3s between shots)
- **Damage**: 1
- **Ammo Capacity**: 30
- **Reload Time**: 1.5s
- **Best For**: Starting weapon, balanced gameplay

### 2. **Machine Gun** 🔪
- **Fire Rate**: Rapid (0.1s between shots)
- **Spread**: 5 degrees
- **Damage**: 1
- **Ammo Capacity**: 60
- **Reload Time**: 2.5s
- **Best For**: Sustained fire against groups

### 3. **Shotgun** 🎯
- **Fire Rate**: Slow (0.8s between shots)
- **Bullets Per Shot**: 6
- **Damage**: 3 per bullet
- **Spread**: 30 degrees
- **Ammo Capacity**: 20
- **Reload Time**: 2.0s
- **Best For**: Close-range devastation

### 4. **Laser Gun** 🔴
- **Fire Rate**: Medium (0.4s between shots)
- **Bullet Speed**: Very Fast (35)
- **Damage**: 2
- **Ammo Capacity**: 50
- **Reload Time**: 1.2s
- **Best For**: Precision shooting

### 5. **Plasma Rifle** 🔵
- **Fire Rate**: Medium (0.5s between shots)
- **Bullets Per Shot**: 2
- **Damage**: 4
- **Spread**: 10 degrees
- **Ammo Capacity**: 40
- **Reload Time**: 2.0s
- **Best For**: Balanced power and speed

### 6. **Sniper Rifle** ⚡
- **Fire Rate**: Slow (1.5s between shots)
- **Bullet Speed**: Extremely Fast (40)
- **Damage**: 5 (Highest)
- **Ammo Capacity**: 15
- **Reload Time**: 3.0s
- **Best For**: One-shot kills

### 7. **Rocket Launcher** 💥
- **Fire Rate**: Slow (1.2s between shots)
- **Damage**: 8 (Massive)
- **Bullet Speed**: Slow (15)
- **Ammo Capacity**: 10
- **Reload Time**: 3.5s
- **Best For**: Group destruction

### 8. **Minigun** 🔥
- **Fire Rate**: Ultra-Rapid (0.05s between shots)
- **Bullets Per Shot**: 3
- **Spread**: 8 degrees
- **Damage**: 1
- **Ammo Capacity**: 200
- **Reload Time**: 4.0s
- **Best For**: Maximum firepower

### 9. **Freeze Ray** ❄️
- **Fire Rate**: Medium (0.6s between shots)
- **Damage**: 2
- **Bullet Speed**: Slow (12)
- **Ammo Capacity**: 35
- **Reload Time**: 2.0s
- **Best For**: Crowd control

## Controls

### Mobile/Touch
- **Tap**: Shoot current gun
- **Drag**: Move player around
- **Swipe Up**: Next weapon
- **Swipe Down**: Previous weapon

### Keyboard (Editor)
- **1-9**: Select specific gun
- **Q**: Previous gun
- **E**: Next gun
- **R**: Reload
- **Space**: Shoot
- **Arrow Keys**: Move

## Tips & Tricks

1. **Weapon Switching**: Switch weapons on the fly for different enemy types
2. **Ammo Management**: Keep an eye on ammo levels
3. **Reload Timing**: Reload during calm moments to avoid being caught empty
4. **Spread Weapons**: Use Machine Gun or Shotgun for groups
5. **Precision Weapons**: Use Laser or Sniper for single targets
6. **Balance**: Mix fast and powerful weapons for versatility
