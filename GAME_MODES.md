# Game Modes - Inspired by Fortnite

Your shooting game features multiple exciting game modes inspired by Fortnite's variety, but with unique mechanics.

## 🎮 Core Game Modes

### 1. **ENDLESS SURVIVOR** (Battle Royale Clone)
- **Objective**: Survive as long as possible
- **Mechanic**: Enemies spawn and descend toward player
- **Unique Twist**: 
  - Shrinking play area (difficulty increases)
  - Boss enemies appear every 50 kills
  - Dynamic weapon drops
  - Score multiplier for streaks
- **Win Condition**: Achieve highest score before death
- **Leaderboard**: Global rank tracking

### 2. **WAVE ASSAULT** (Arena/Horde Mode)
- **Objective**: Survive increasing waves of enemies
- **Mechanic**: Waves get progressively harder
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Special power-ups between waves
  - Ammo regeneration stations
  - Limited time to prepare between waves
  - Boss wave every 5th wave
- **Win Condition**: Survive all 10 waves
- **Reward**: Bonus currency & achievements

### 3. **TACTICAL EXTRACTION** (Mission Mode)
- **Objective**: Destroy specific enemy targets
- **Mechanic**: Hunt down marked enemies with time limits
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Limited ammo for each mission
  - Environmental hazards
  - Rescue NPCs for bonus points
  - Multiple difficulty tiers
- **Win Condition**: Complete all objectives before time runs out
- **Reward**: Exclusive cosmetics

### 4. **DUELS** (1v1 PvP Mode)
- **Objective**: Eliminate opponent first
- **Mechanic**: Direct player-vs-player combat
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Weapon randomization each round
  - Power-up pickups in arena
  - Best-of-5 rounds
  - Ranked rating system
- **Win Condition**: Win 3+ rounds
- **Reward**: Ranked points & cosmetics

### 5. **SQUAD BLITZ** (Team Mode)
- **Objective**: Team vs Enemy waves
- **Mechanic**: Play with up to 4 squad mates
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Shared resource pool (ammo/health)
  - Team combo multipliers
  - Leader abilities
  - Respawn system (revive teammates)
- **Win Condition**: Survive longest as team
- **Reward**: Team cosmetics & squad challenges

### 6. **FORTRESS DEFENSE** (Tower Defense)
- **Objective**: Defend base from enemy waves
- **Mechanic**: Build defenses between waves
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Turret placement system
  - Wall construction
  - Gate reinforcement
  - Limited building resources
- **Win Condition**: Defend base for 15 waves
- **Reward**: Builder cosmetics

### 7. **CHAOS MODE** (Fast-Paced Arcade)
- **Objective**: Maximum kills in 5 minutes
- **Mechanic**: Endless fast-spawning enemies
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Gun changes every 30 seconds (forced)
  - Double damage modifiers
  - Explosive difficulty scaling
  - Constant visual effects
- **Win Condition**: Highest kill count wins
- **Reward**: Speed cosmetics & speed challenges

### 8. **TREASURE HUNT** (Adventure Mode)
- **Objective**: Collect scattered treasure while fighting
- **Mechanic**: Hunt treasure across expanding map
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Clue system to find treasure
  - Rival hunters competing
  - Secret passages
  - Treasure chest bosses
- **Win Condition**: Collect 5 treasures
- **Reward**: Pirate cosmetics & exclusive skins

### 9. **ELIMINATION RUMBLE** (Multi-Player Chaos)
- **Objective**: Last player standing
- **Mechanic**: Free-for-all with 8+ players
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Random weapon drops
  - Environmental damage
  - Shrinking arena
  - Revenge bonus (kill your killer = bonus points)
- **Win Condition**: Be last one alive
- **Reward**: Survivor cosmetics

### 10. **BOSS RAID** (Cooperative Dungeon)
- **Objective**: Defeat increasingly powerful bosses
- **Mechanic**: Co-op boss battles
- **Unique Twist**:
  - 4-player raids
  - Boss phases with mechanics
  - Special abilities to use
  - Loot drops for each phase
- **Win Condition**: Defeat final boss
- **Reward**: Legendary cosmetics & weapons

### 11. **DAILY CHALLENGE** (Roguelike)
- **Objective**: Complete random challenges
- **Mechanic**: Procedurally generated objectives
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Permadeath runs
  - Difficulty modifiers
  - Artifact collection
  - Meta progression
- **Win Condition**: Complete all 5 objectives in one run
- **Reward**: Battle pass points & cosmetics

### 12. **SPEED RUN** (Time Attack)
- **Objective**: Kill set enemies as fast as possible
- **Mechanic**: Optimized combat runs
- **Unique Twist**:
  - Leaderboard times
  - Different difficulty presets
  - Multiple challenge routes
  - Personal bests tracking
- **Win Condition**: Fastest completion time
- **Reward**: Speed cosmetics & global rankings

## 📊 Progression Systems

### Experience & Leveling
- Gain XP across all modes
- Unlock new weapons at level milestones
- Cosmetic rewards every 5 levels

### Battle Pass
- Free & Premium tracks
- Weekly challenges to progress faster
- Exclusive cosmetics & emotes

### Ranked System (Duels & Rumble)
- Placement matches to determine rank
- Tier-based: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Master
- Seasonal resets
- Exclusive rewards per tier

### Daily/Weekly Challenges
- 3 daily challenges (500 XP each)
- 4 weekly challenges (2000 XP each)
- Special event challenges
- Secret challenges with bonus rewards

## 🎯 Game Mode Strategy

**For Beginners**: Start with Wave Assault to learn mechanics
**For Competitive**: Try Duels for ranked progression
**For Relaxation**: Play Endless Survivor at your own pace
**For Challenge**: Attempt Boss Raid with squad
**For Speed**: Compete in Speed Run leaderboards

Each mode offers unique gameplay, rewards, and cosmetics to unlock!
